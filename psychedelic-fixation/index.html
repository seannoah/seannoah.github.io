Eye-tracking demo · mouse edition

Psychedelic Fixation

In the lab this runs on a Tobii eye tracker. Wherever you hold your gaze, hallucination-like distortions bloom outward from that point and grow stronger the longer you stay still.

Here, your mouse pointer stands in for your eyes. Rest it anywhere, look right at it, and keep it still. Move it, and a new bloom starts there; the old spot stops producing anything new, and its ripples travel outward and fade.

Contains swirling motion and rapidly shifting colours.

Each bloom sends out waves for as long as you hold still. They travel at a steady speed across V1, so in your visual field they speed up and widen as they move out. Look away and the old spot stops sending: its waves travel on and fade. The V1 view (C) shows the scene around your gaze laid out the way V1 maps it, with the expanding ripples as straight stripes.
