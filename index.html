About

I study how the brain builds perceptual experience. I'm particularly interested in active processes such as attention and prediction, and the role they play in generating conscious experience.

My work combines EEG, behavioral experiments, and computational analysis, along with causal manipulations such as psychoactive compounds, to ask what perception is made of and how it can be changed.

Research

  • Attention and neural dynamics

    EEG decoding of how attended and ignored object representations compete and evolve over time.

  • Perceptual multistability

    How the visual system selects one interpretation of an ambiguous image, and what holds it in place.

  • Psychedelic perception

    Mapping altered visual experience by combining neuroimaging and behavior with large-scale language analysis of first-person reports.
